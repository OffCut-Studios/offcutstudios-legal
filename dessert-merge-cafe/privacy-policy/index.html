OffCut Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Dessert Merge Cafe (the "Game") for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, stored, and deleted when you use the Game, its online features, and our player-support services.

1. Information We Process

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in through Google Play Games, Google and Unity Authentication process account identifiers and authentication information needed to connect you to your game account. These can include your Google Play Games player identifier, Unity player identifier, and authentication/session tokens. We do not receive your Google account password.

Gameplay progress and cloud backups

The Game stores progress locally on your device. When your account is signed in and cloud saving is available, the Game automatically uploads eligible progress to Unity Cloud Save. This information includes your cafe name, currency balance, lifetime earnings and completed-order totals, rebirth progress, upgrades, board and shelf contents, customer orders, dessert collection, tutorial progress, energy state, and information needed to validate and restore a save.

Cloud records also contain account identifiers, save checksums and synchronization information needed to associate progress with the correct player and manage conflicting saves. The Game uses Unity Cloud Code to process saved totals for leaderboard publication.

Public leaderboard information

The Game publishes your cafe name, lifetime money earned, lifetime orders completed, current rebirth, and ranking to Unity Leaderboards. Other signed-in players can view the cafe name, relevant total, rebirth, and rank for each entry. Leaderboard records are associated with a Unity player identifier.

💡

Public Cafe Name Notice: Please choose a cafe name you are comfortable making public. Do not include your real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in it. Your full gameplay backup and authentication tokens are not displayed in the leaderboard interface.

Device, connection, and diagnostic information

The Game's service providers may process information such as IP address, installation or device identifiers, device model, operating system, app version, language, network information, request timestamps, and error or diagnostic information. The information processed depends on the service, device settings, and privacy choices that apply.

Optional analytics

The Game includes Unity Analytics and a separate analytics consent choice. If you enable analytics, Unity may process installation identifiers, technical device information, application lifecycle events, session information, and IP address to help us understand use of the Game and improve its operation.

You can change your analytics choice in the Game's Settings. Declining optional analytics does not prevent normal gameplay. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection through the Game's analytics consent controls; it does not automatically delete information already received by Unity or cancel an upload already in progress. You can request deletion of previously collected information as described below.

Advertising information

The Game displays advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. Ads include optional rewarded advertisements for energy or cash bonuses, and interstitial advertisements that may appear when returning to the home screen.

Advertising services may process Android Advertising ID when available, other device or installation identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and network information, ad requests, ad views, clicks, reward-completion events, and diagnostic information. These services use data for advertising delivery, measurement, frequency management, security, fraud prevention, and, where applicable, personalization.

Before initializing advertising, the Game asks for an age group: under 13, 13–17, or 18 and above. This age group and your advertising choice are stored locally, separately from your cloud save and analytics choice; we do not ask for your date of birth. Advertising is unavailable for the under-13 group.

For ages 13–17, the Game requests restricted treatment from Unity, disables personalized advertising, signals no sale/sharing and requests that the ad SDK does not access the Advertising ID. Adults can choose limited ads or explicitly allow personalized ads. Limited ads use the same non-personalized and identifier-restriction signals. These signals do not eliminate all technical processing needed to deliver an ad.

Review or change these choices through Settings → Advertising privacy. Choosing limited ads withdraws the Game's personalized-ad consent signal and clears previously loaded ad inventory. It does not reverse information already processed by a provider. Normal gameplay does not require personalized ads.

Advertising services can process information when their SDK initializes or requests an advertisement, even if you do not choose to watch a rewarded ad. The analytics consent setting does not control advertising data processing. Unity and its advertising partners describe their processing and available choices in the policies linked in Section 4.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive the email address you use, your message, and any information you choose to provide, such as a game identifier or diagnostic details. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information, or identity documents when contacting support.

2. Why Information Is Used

We and the relevant service providers process information to:

  • Operate the Game and preserve gameplay progress.
  • Authenticate players and associate cloud progress with the correct account.
  • Back up and restore progress, including after reinstalling the Game.
  • Publish and display leaderboard rankings.
  • Deliver and measure advertising and grant earned ad rewards.
  • Understand app use through optional analytics when enabled.
  • Diagnose errors, secure services, and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide a requested service, pursue legitimate interests such as security and support, comply with legal obligations, or act on consent. Processing that legally requires consent must be based on valid consent; this policy is not itself a request for, or evidence of, that consent.

3. Local Storage, Cloud Storage & Connectivity

Local saves and cloud backups are separate. Some gameplay can continue without a connection after the Game is ready, but sign-in, cloud synchronization, leaderboards, and advertising depend on network and service availability.

On Device

Local Save Progress

Progress remains locally on your device. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the Game can remove local progress.

Cloud Services

Cloud Backups & Accounts

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not by itself delete cloud backups, authentication records, leaderboard entries, or service provider records. Signing out also does not delete those records.

5. Your Choices

  • Review and change optional analytics consent in the Game's Settings.
  • Use Android's privacy or advertising settings to manage or delete your Advertising ID where supported. The available settings depend on Android and Google Play services versions. This does not necessarily stop all advertising or all processing needed to deliver contextual ads.
  • Use privacy options provided by Unity or within advertisements where available.
  • Avoid personal information in your public cafe name.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, account deletion, or data deletion.

Advertising-related disclosures or transfers may qualify as a "sale", "sharing", or targeted advertising under some regional laws, even when no money is paid for the information. Where those laws apply, you may exercise the relevant opt-out rights through available provider controls and by contacting us. Unity's privacy choices apply to its services; they do not automatically govern every other provider's processing.

6. Retention

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed, overwritten, or reset. We retain account-linked cloud progress and leaderboard information for as long as needed to provide the corresponding features, unless you request deletion or another deletion requirement applies.

Support correspondence and request records are retained only as needed to handle the request, maintain appropriate operational records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Service providers may maintain their own retention schedules for information they process under their own policies.

Deletion can require additional processing time for provider systems and backup copies. If particular information must be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention reasons, we limit it to the necessary purpose and explain applicable exceptions in responding to your request.

7. Account & Data Deletion

In the Game, open Settings → Request account / data deletion to view the instructions and prepare a support email containing your available game player identifier. Review and send the email to submit your request. Opening the request screen, website, or email composer alone does not delete your account.

You can request deletion without reinstalling the Game by emailing studiosoffcut@gmail.com, preferably with the subject Dessert Merge Cafe — Account and Data Deletion.

Tell us whether you want to delete your game account and associated data, specific cloud progress, leaderboard information, or previously collected analytics data. Provide a game identifier if available. If you cannot access the Game or find an identifier, contact us for assistance. We may request additional non-sensitive information to locate the records and verify that you are authorized to request their deletion; a public identifier alone may not establish account ownership.

For an account-deletion request, we will arrange deletion of the associated game account and relevant cloud progress, summary records, and leaderboard entries, and coordinate applicable provider deletion requests. We will respond without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law, including explaining any permitted retention exceptions. Deletion can be irreversible and may prevent recovery of your progress.

Game-account deletion does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile. Manage those accounts through Google. Locally retained progress may remain on your device; clear app storage if you also want to remove that copy. Continuing to use the Game or signing in again can create new records.

Further instructions are available on our Account and Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Where a higher age for valid consent applies, the relevant local requirements also apply. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us at studiosoffcut@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required. This statement does not replace any age or parental-consent safeguards required for the Game's actual audience.

9. Security & International Processing

The Game uses HTTPS/TLS for service communications and account-based controls for cloud access. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Unity, Google, and other relevant providers may process information outside your country, including in countries with different data-protection laws. Applicable transfer safeguards depend on the recipient and service; consult the linked provider policies for their international-processing arrangements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising disclosures. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority and, where applicable, appeal a decision concerning a privacy request.

Contact studiosoffcut@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Game, services, or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required for material changes.
