Privacy Policy

Dessert Merge Cafe | Offcut Studios

Status: Draft — not effective

This document describes planned privacy practices. It is not the final privacy policy for a released version of Dessert Merge Cafe.

1. Introduction

Offcut Studios develops Dessert Merge Cafe, a mobile game for Android.

This draft describes how player information is expected to be processed when the planned online services are implemented.

2. Information We Plan to Process

  • Google Play Games player identifiers, display names, and authentication information.
  • Gameplay progress, virtual currency balances, upgrades, and unlocked content.
  • Leaderboard scores and rankings.
  • Device identifiers, technical information, and diagnostic information.
  • Gameplay analytics and session events, subject to applicable privacy choices.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interactions, subject to applicable consent requirements.

3. How Information Will Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate players.
  • Save and restore gameplay progress.
  • Maintain global leaderboards.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Analyze gameplay and improve the Game.
  • Maintain security and resolve technical issues.
  • Respond to privacy and deletion requests.

4. Google Play Games

Dessert Merge Cafe plans to require Google Play Games authentication before gameplay.

Google Play Games is also planned for global leaderboards, including total money earned. Depending on provider settings, player display names, scores, and rankings may be visible to other players.

5. Firebase Cloud Saves

Dessert Merge Cafe plans to use Firebase Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize player progress.

Cloud-save records may contain account-linked identifiers, saved progress, currency balances, unlocked content, upgrade levels, save versions, and synchronization metadata.

These records are intended to remain private and accessible only through authorized access.

6. Advertising and Consent

The Game plans to display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and advertisement interactions.

Offcut Studios plans to use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

Applicable age-related advertising safeguards will be implemented before release.

7. Unity Analytics

The Game plans to use Unity Analytics to understand gameplay activity and improve the player experience.

Players will be provided with an in-game privacy control for optional analytics. Consent will be obtained where required.

Players will be able to withdraw applicable consent and request deletion of eligible analytics information.

8. Third-Party Services

Planned service providers include:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Unity Analytics

9. Data Security

Offcut Studios intends to implement appropriate authentication, access restrictions, and secure data transmission to protect player information. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Players will be offered in-game and external methods for requesting deletion of eligible game-related information.

Requests may also be sent to studiosoffcut@gmail.com .

Applicable account-linked cloud records will be deleted following verified requests, subject to legitimate retention exceptions. Retention periods and deletion procedures must be finalized before release.

Deleting game-related information does not automatically delete a player's Google account or Google Play Games profile.

11. Children's Privacy

Dessert Merge Cafe is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not intentionally directed toward children under 13.

Applicable age-related privacy and advertising requirements will be addressed before release.

12. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the player's country of residence by applicable service providers, subject to relevant legal requirements and safeguards.

13. Player Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, players may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of their personal information, and to withdraw applicable consent.

Privacy requests can be submitted to studiosoffcut@gmail.com .

14. Policy Changes

Offcut Studios may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game, service providers, data practices, or applicable legal requirements.
