1. Introduction

OffCut Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Dessert Merge Cafe, a mobile casual merge game for Android available on Google Play.

We are committed to transparent privacy practices and protecting player data. This Privacy Policy explains our current data handling practices as well as the planned privacy architecture for upcoming online features.

If you have any questions or privacy-related requests, you can reach our team at studiosoffcut@gmail.com.

2. Architecture & Status

To ensure complete clarity regarding what data is processed today versus what is planned for future updates:

Active Gameplay

Local Save V2 System

Operates offline on Unity 6. Gameplay progress, board configurations, coin totals, and unlocked merge items remain strictly on your local device.

Retired

Firebase Not Integrated

No Firebase package, application configuration, or source integration is currently verified. Any legacy Firebase SDKs previously used should be treated as retired; Firebase is not part of the current implementation.

Uninitialized

Unity Analytics (v6.3.0)

Installed with separate consent controls in the codebase. Runtime initialization and collection remain subject to a player's explicit Analytics choice and require verification in released builds.

Planned Services

Upcoming Online Features

Google Play Games sign-in and Unity Authentication code exists, but mandatory startup authentication and an operational cloud-save architecture are not verified. Unity Cloud Save, advertising activation, and other online features require separate implementation and deployment verification.

3. Data Categories

When planned online services are finalized and deployed, Dessert Merge Cafe may process the following categories of information:

  • Authentication Identifiers: When Google Play Games or Unity Authentication is used, Google Play Games player or account identifiers and the associated Unity Authentication player identifier may be processed for authentication and account-session functionality.
  • Game Progress & Backup State: Saved game data (e.g., merge board state, currency balances, unlocked dessert recipes, cafe upgrade levels, and save timestamps) synced to Unity Cloud Save for recovery and backup.
  • Device & Technical Information: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, screen resolution, language/locale settings, and IP address (used by service infrastructure for coarse geographic routing and connection handling).
  • Analytics Telemetry (Subject to Explicit Opt-In): Standard application lifecycle and session events (such as session start and duration) processed by Unity Analytics only if you grant explicit consent.
  • Advertising Data (Planned and Subject to Verification): When active advertising is enabled, Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, approximate location, ad requests, advertisement interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices, subject to applicable consent and regional requirements. Exact SDK data practices and integration status require verification before publication of this disclosure.

4. How Data Is Used

Information processed by planned services will be used solely for legitimate game operations:

  • Authenticating players through Google Play Games and Unity Authentication where the service architecture requires or offers a verified account session.
  • Backing up and restoring gameplay progress using Unity Cloud Save.
  • Providing customer support and processing player data deletion requests.
  • Understanding application stability and aggregated session trends (if analytics consent is granted).
  • Delivering advertisements to support game development (once advertising solutions are finalized and implemented).
  • Ensuring technical security, preventing abuse, and complying with legal obligations.

5. Save V2 vs Cloud Save

It is important to understand the boundary between your local gameplay save and planned cloud backups:

  • Local Save V2 (Active): The primary game save exists entirely on your Android device. It operates without an account or internet connection and stores your complete gameplay progression locally.
  • Unity Cloud Save (Planned Backup Layer): Unity Cloud Save is not yet verified as operational. If implemented, it may serve as an online backup and recovery layer after successful Google Play Games sign-in and Unity Authentication.
  • Identity Separation: Your Unity Authentication record (managed within Unity Gaming Services) and your Google Play Games profile (managed by Google LLC) remain distinct. Deleting game-related data does not delete your Google account or your Google Play Games profile.

6. Google Play Games

Dessert Merge Cafe includes Google Play Games and Unity Authentication integration for account-based features. The intended architecture is online authentication for features that require a verified player session.

Manual sign-in, cached-session restoration, and account status presentation are implemented in the account settings area. Mandatory startup authentication and gameplay-wide enforcement have not been verified. Until those behaviors are confirmed, this policy does not guarantee that every gameplay mode requires online sign-in. Any future Firebase or Unity Cloud Save functionality must be separately verified and disclosed; Firebase is not currently part of the implemented integration.

Google Play Games Services is operated by Google LLC and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Cloud Architecture

The planned cloud recovery architecture operates through the following sequence:

  1. Account Sign-in
    For account-based features, the player initiates sign-in using Google Play Games Services. Mandatory startup enforcement remains subject to implementation and verification.
  2. Federated Identity
    Credentials are federated securely to Unity Authentication to establish a unique Unity player identifier.
  3. Automatic Backup Sync
    Unity Cloud Save, if separately implemented, would need verification before it can back up progress to Unity Gaming Services servers.

Cloud Save is intended strictly as a backup and recovery layer; local Save V2 remains protected as the primary local storage. Cloud-saved records are private to your authenticated identity and are used exclusively to restore progress across device reinstalls or hardware changes.

This architecture is planned and not currently active or operational in released builds.

8. Unity Analytics

Unity Analytics 6.3.0 and separate Analytics consent controls are present in the project code. Runtime initialization and collection depend on the player's explicit choice and require deployment verification. Analytics functionality follows the Unity 6 Developer Data consent framework and the following privacy requirements:

  • Explicit Opt-In Required: Analytics collection is completely optional and disabled by default. It requires your explicit, informed consent before any data collection starts.
  • No Gameplay Blocking: Declining analytics consent will never block, degrade, or restrict your gameplay experience in any way.
  • Configurable in Settings: Once implemented, you will be able to review, grant, or withdraw your analytics consent at any time within the in-game Settings menu.
  • Data Scope: When consented, Unity Analytics may process device technical specifications, session duration, app lifecycle events, installation identifiers, and IP addresses (which Unity uses for coarse geographic estimation before standard processing).
  • No Custom Gameplay Events in Initial Scope: The initial integration includes no custom gameplay tracking events; only standard automated session and lifecycle telemetry will be processed if consent is provided.
  • Withdrawing Consent: If you withdraw consent, the Unity Analytics SDK stops future data collection under its developer consent controls. Please note:
    • The SDK may attempt to transmit previously buffered events that were recorded prior to withdrawal.
    • We do not promise that withdrawal cancels an in-flight network upload.
    • Withdrawing consent stops future collection but does not automatically delete data previously received by Unity.
    • Requests to delete previously collected analytics data are handled separately through our data deletion process.

9. Advertising

Dessert Merge Cafe published by OffCut Studios may use Unity Ads mediated through Unity LevelPlay.

Current advertising status:

  • Unity LevelPlay 9.6.0 and its Unity Ads adapter are installed, while ad units and controlled Android test setup remain subject to Inspector, dashboard, and device verification.
  • Active advertising is not authorized for public release. Development test advertisements are intended only for controlled adult test sessions, contain no gameplay rewards, and are manually triggered.
  • The game is intended for players aged 13 and above. Exclusion of advertisements containing 18+ content is pending Unity Ads dashboard verification.
  • Advertising may require separate regional consent and an approved privacy disclosure. Unity Analytics consent does not represent advertising consent.
  • Where advertising is active and permitted, Unity Ads and LevelPlay may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, approximate location, advertisement requests, interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices. Exact data practices must be verified against the installed SDK and applicable documentation before publication.

Players may accept or decline advertising where the advertising privacy controls are presented. Declining or withdrawing does not prevent normal gameplay. Withdrawal blocks subsequent app-controlled advertising initialization, requests, and displays, but cannot undo previously processed data or guarantee that an advertisement already showing will stop immediately.

11. Children's Privacy

Dessert Merge Cafe is intended for players aged 13 and above. It is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without verified parental consent.

Google Play target-age designations and age-gating mechanisms are undergoing formal review prior to release. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without required parental consent, please contact us immediately at studiosoffcut@gmail.com. Upon notification, we will take prompt steps to verify and delete such information from our records.

12. Data Security

We are dedicated to safeguarding your information. When online services are active, technical and organizational safeguards will include:

  • Encrypted communications over the network using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Restricted administrative access to cloud consoles and database records.
  • Reliance on enterprise-grade infrastructure managed by Google and Unity Technologies.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

15. Player Rights

Depending on your region of residence (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, or other jurisdictions with applicable privacy laws), you may have certain statutory rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation of whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): Request deletion of personal information held in our cloud systems.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent previously provided for optional processing such as Unity Analytics or advertising, where the relevant controls are available, without affecting gameplay.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Receive equal game service regardless of exercising your privacy rights.

To submit a privacy inquiry or exercise any of these rights, please contact us at studiosoffcut@gmail.com.
