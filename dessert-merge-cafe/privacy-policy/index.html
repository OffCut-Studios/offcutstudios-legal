DRAFT NOTICE: This document describes planned privacy practices for Dessert Merge Cafe. It is currently in DRAFT status and is not effective for released builds of the game.

The current Android game operates locally using the on-device Save V2 system. Firebase SDKs have been completely retired. While the Unity Analytics SDK (v6.3.0) is present in the codebase, it is not initialized, and no analytics collection is authorized or active. Planned online services—including Google Play Games Services, Unity Authentication, Unity Cloud Save, Unity Analytics, and Unity Ads—are not currently operational.

1. Introduction

OffCut Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Dessert Merge Cafe, a mobile casual merge game for Android available on Google Play.

We are committed to transparent privacy practices and protecting player data. This Privacy Policy explains our current data handling practices as well as the planned privacy architecture for upcoming online features.

If you have any questions or privacy-related requests, you can contact us at studiosoffcut@gmail.com.

2. Current Game Architecture vs. Planned Services

To ensure complete clarity regarding what data is processed today versus what is planned for future updates:

  • Current Game State: The current game runs on Unity 6 for Android and uses an on-device local save system (Save V2). Gameplay progress, coin totals, and unlocked merge items remain strictly on your local device.
  • Retired Services: All Firebase SDKs (including Firebase Firestore and Firebase Cloud Save) have been retired and removed. No data is collected or stored using Firebase.
  • Installed but Inactive SDKs: The Unity Analytics package (version 6.3.0) is installed within the game project but is not initialized at runtime. No analytics data collection is authorized or active in current builds.
  • Planned Online Services: Planned features include optional player sign-in via Google Play Games Services, account federation via Unity Authentication, automatic progress backup via Unity Cloud Save, optional analytics via Unity Analytics, and advertising via Unity Ads. None of these services are operational at this stage.

3. Information We Plan to Process

When planned online services are finalized and deployed, Dessert Merge Cafe may process the following categories of information:

  • Authentication Identifiers: Google Play Games player identifiers and display names (if you choose to sign in), along with an associated Unity Authentication player identifier generated by Unity Gaming Services.
  • Game Progress & State: Saved game data (e.g., merge board state, currency balances, unlocked dessert recipes, cafe upgrade levels, and save timestamps) synced to Unity Cloud Save for recovery and backup.
  • Leaderboard Information: Optional leaderboard scores and rankings submitted through Google Play Games Services.
  • Device & Technical Information: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, screen resolution, language/locale settings, and IP address (used by service infrastructure for coarse geographic routing and connection handling).
  • Analytics Telemetry (Subject to Explicit Opt-In): Standard application lifecycle and session events (such as session start and duration) processed by Unity Analytics only if you grant explicit consent.
  • Advertising Data (Planned): Device identifiers and ad interaction signals, subject to applicable consent requirements and verification of the advertising SDK.

4. How Information Will Be Used

Information processed by planned services will be used solely for legitimate game operations:

  • Authenticating players across sessions via Google Play Games.
  • Backing up and restoring gameplay progress using Unity Cloud Save.
  • Displaying public leaderboard standings via Google Play Games.
  • Providing customer support and processing player data deletion requests.
  • Understanding application stability and aggregated session trends (if analytics consent is granted).
  • Delivering advertisements to support game development (once advertising solutions are finalized and implemented).
  • Ensuring technical security, preventing abuse, and complying with legal obligations.

5. Save Systems: Local Save V2 & Planned Cloud Save

It is important to understand the boundary between your local gameplay save and planned cloud backups:

  • Local Save V2 (Active): The primary game save exists entirely on your Android device. It operates without an account or internet connection and stores your complete gameplay progression locally.
  • Unity Cloud Save (Planned Backup Layer): Once implemented, Cloud Save will serve as a secondary backup and recovery layer. It will activate automatically only after successful Google Play Games sign-in and Unity Authentication. Cloud Save is designed to protect your progress without replacing or corrupting your local Save V2 data.
  • Identity Separation: Your Unity Authentication record (managed within Unity Gaming Services) and your Google Play Games profile (managed by Google LLC) remain distinct. Deleting game-related data does not delete your Google account or your Google Play Games profile.

6. Google Play Games Services (Planned)

Dessert Merge Cafe plans to offer player sign-in through Google Play Games Services.

Signing in will allow the game to link your Google Play Games identity to Unity Authentication to facilitate automatic cloud backup and optional leaderboard participation. If you participate in leaderboards, your Google Play Games display name and score may be visible to other players based on your Google Play Games profile settings.

Google Play Games Services is operated by Google LLC and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Unity Authentication and Cloud Save Architecture (Planned)

The planned cloud recovery architecture operates through the following chain:

  1. Player initiates sign-in using Google Play Games Services.
  2. Credentials are federated securely to Unity Authentication to establish a unique Unity player identifier.
  3. Unity Cloud Save is automatically enabled, syncing a backup snapshot of your game progress to Unity Gaming Services servers.

Cloud-saved records are private to your authenticated identity and are used exclusively to restore progress across device reinstalls or hardware changes.

8. Unity Analytics and Consent Choices (Planned)

Unity Analytics 6.3.0 is included in the game build but is currently uninitialized. When analytics functionality is released, the following strict privacy principles will apply:

  • Explicit Opt-In Required: Analytics collection is completely optional and will require your explicit, informed consent before any data collection starts.
  • No Gameplay Blocking: Declining analytics consent will never block, degrade, or restrict your gameplay experience in any way.
  • Configurable in Settings: Once implemented, you will be able to review, grant, or withdraw your analytics consent at any time within the in-game Settings menu.
  • Data Scope: When consented, Unity Analytics may process device technical specifications, session duration, app lifecycle events, installation identifiers, and IP addresses (which Unity uses for coarse geographic estimation before standard processing).
  • No Custom Gameplay Events in Initial Scope: The initial integration includes no custom gameplay tracking events; only standard automated session and lifecycle telemetry will be processed if consent is provided.
  • Withdrawing Consent: If you withdraw consent, the Unity Analytics SDK stops future data collection under its built-in consent controls. Please note:
    • The SDK may attempt to transmit previously buffered events that were recorded prior to withdrawal.
    • We do not promise that withdrawal cancels an in-flight network upload.
    • Withdrawing consent stops future collection but does not automatically delete data previously received by Unity.
    • Requests to delete previously collected analytics data are handled separately through our data deletion process.

12. Data Security

We are dedicated to safeguarding your information. When online services are active, technical and organizational safeguards will include:

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
