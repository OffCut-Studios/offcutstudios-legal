Data Deletion Requests

Dessert Merge Cafe

Published by: OffCut Studios • Last Updated: October 2026 (Draft)
⚠️

DRAFT NOTICE: This page describes the planned data deletion procedure for Dessert Merge Cafe. It is currently in DRAFT status and is not effective for released builds.

The current game operates locally with the on-device Save V2 system. Firebase SDKs have been completely retired. Automated in-game deletion and cloud backend services are currently pending release and implementation. Data deletion requests are handled manually via email as detailed below.

1. Overview

OffCut Studios is committed to giving players meaningful control over their personal data. We provide mechanisms for players to request the deletion of eligible game-related data associated with Dessert Merge Cafe.

Currently, Dessert Merge Cafe operates locally using an offline save system (Save V2). As planned online services—including Google Play Games Services, Unity Authentication, Unity Cloud Save, and Unity Analytics—are introduced, cloud-stored records will be eligible for deletion upon verified request.

2. Understanding Data Categories and Storage

To understand how deletion applies to your data, it is necessary to distinguish between local device data, cloud services, and third-party account platforms:

On Device

Local Save V2 Progress

Your primary game save file (board layout, coins, and recipes) resides directly on your Android device. OffCut Studios has no remote access to your device.

Delete anytime via: Settings > Apps > Dessert Merge Cafe > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Planned Cloud

Unity Cloud Save & Auth

Once implemented, backup snapshots of progress and credentials will reside in Unity Gaming Services linked to a Unity player ID.

Eligible for complete permanent erasure upon email request.

Planned Analytics

Unity Analytics Telemetry

If opted in under the Unity 6 Developer Data consent model, technical session events are collected.

Withdrawing consent stops future collection. Deleting past data requires an email deletion request.

External Account

Google Play Games

Optional sign-in platform managed by Google LLC. Deleting game data from OffCut Studios does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile.

Manage profile directly via Google Account privacy settings.

3. How to Submit a Data Deletion Request

To request the deletion of your cloud-stored game records or analytics data, submit a request by email:

  1. Send an Email
    Send an email to studiosoffcut@gmail.com.
  2. Include Standard Subject
    Use the exact subject line: Dessert Merge Cafe - Data Deletion Request.
  3. Specify the Scope
    In your email, clarify what records you wish to purge:
    • Delete all cloud-saved game progress (Unity Cloud Save & Unity Authentication).
    • Delete historical analytics telemetry (Unity Analytics).
    • Delete all eligible cloud records across all services.
  4. Provide In-Game Identifier
    Include your player identifier (such as your Unity Player ID or Analytics ID, which will be accessible in the in-game Settings menu once online features are implemented) to allow us to locate your records.
🔒

IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE: For your protection, never include passwords, Google account credentials, credit card numbers, government identification cards, or other sensitive personal documentation in your email. OffCut Studios will never ask for your passwords or identity documents to fulfill a deletion request.

4. Operational Deletion Workflow

Because automated client-side deletion is not yet deployed, OffCut Studios manually processes and coordinates data deletion requests. Our operational fulfillment steps are as follows:

  1. Receipt and Verification
    We log your deletion request and verify the provided player identifier against our backend systems. If further clarification is needed to locate your records, we will reply to your email without requesting sensitive information.
  2. Cloud Save & Account Deletion
    For requests covering cloud progress, an administrator initiates the permanent deletion of player save data, state snapshots, and associated account credentials in the Unity Gaming Services dashboard and administrative APIs.
  3. Analytics Data Erasure
    For requests covering analytics, we submit a formal data erasure request through Unity's privacy and data management tools for the identified player or installation identifier.
  4. Completion Confirmation
    Once backend processing is finalized by the respective service providers, we will send an email confirmation that your eligible data has been purged.

Requests are reviewed and processed reasonably without undue delay in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

5. Status of In-Game Deletion Features

Please note the current development status regarding in-game controls:

  • An automated in-game deletion button is not currently implemented in the active game.
  • Unity's programmatic deletion API has not yet been integrated into the game client.
  • Consent toggles in game Settings (planned for analytics) allow you to withdraw consent from future data collection, but purging historical data requires submitting an email request as outlined above.

6. Retention Exceptions

When you request data deletion, eligible cloud game saves and analytics records will be deleted. However, certain limited information may be retained where strictly necessary to:

  • Demonstrate compliance with applicable data protection regulations (such as keeping a record of the fulfilled deletion request).
  • Resolve ongoing legal disputes or enforce our agreements.
  • Satisfy specific statutory, tax, or legal retention obligations under applicable law.
