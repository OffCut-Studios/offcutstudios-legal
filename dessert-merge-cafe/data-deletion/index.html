Data Deletion Requests

Dessert Merge Cafe | Offcut Studios

Status: Draft — deletion workflow pending implementation

This page describes the planned data deletion process for Dessert Merge Cafe. Automated account and cloud-data deletion is not yet available.

1. Overview

Offcut Studios is committed to giving players appropriate control over their game-related data.

Dessert Merge Cafe plans to use Google Play Games for player authentication and Firebase Cloud Firestore for cloud-saved progress.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

You can contact Offcut Studios to ask about deletion of your Dessert Merge Cafe data.

  1. Email studiosoffcut@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject: Dessert Merge Cafe - Data Deletion Request .
  3. Explain whether you want to delete your game account and associated data, or request deletion of specific eligible data.
  4. Follow any reasonable identity-verification instructions provided by Offcut Studios.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, government identification documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Planned In-Game Deletion

Dessert Merge Cafe plans to provide an in-game option for requesting deletion of game-related account information and saved progress.

This functionality is not yet implemented.

4. Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the services implemented and the scope of a verified request, eligible information may include:

  • Game account association records.
  • Firebase cloud-saved player progress.
  • Stored virtual currency and upgrade data.
  • Unlocked content and progression records.
  • Eligible account-linked metadata.

5. Third-Party Information

Deleting Dessert Merge Cafe game-related information does not automatically delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile.

Information held independently by Google, Unity, or other service providers may be subject to their separate privacy and deletion procedures.

6. Retention and Processing

Offcut Studios will establish appropriate verification, deletion, and retention procedures before releasing the planned account and cloud-save features.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

Specific retention periods and request processing timelines have not yet been finalized.

7. Privacy Policy

For more information, see the Dessert Merge Cafe Privacy Policy .
