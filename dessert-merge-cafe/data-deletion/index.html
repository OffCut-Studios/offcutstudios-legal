You can request deletion of your Dessert Merge Cafe game account and associated data without reinstalling the Game.

1. Submit a Request

To request deletion, you may submit a request directly through the Game or by email:

  1. In-Game Request Screen
    In the Game, navigating to Settings → Request account / data deletion opens instructions and can prepare a pre-formatted request email populated with your available player identifier. Merely opening the screen or email composer does not submit a request or delete anything; you must review and send the email.
  2. Email Submission
    Email studiosoffcut@gmail.com with the subject Dessert Merge Cafe — Account and Data Deletion.
  3. Specify Deletion Scope
    Tell us what you want deleted. A complete game-account request covers:
    • Unity Authentication game account
    • Unity Cloud Save backups and summary fields
    • Public leaderboard entries
    • Applicable analytics data
    You can also ask about deleting specific individual records.
  4. Provide Player Identifier
    Include your game player identifier if available. If you cannot find it or no longer have the Game installed, contact us and we will help locate your records.
🔒

IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE: Never send a Google account password, authentication token, payment-card information, or identity document. OffCut Studios will never ask for your passwords or sensitive personal documents to fulfill a deletion request.

2. Verification & Completion

We verify your authority to request deletion before acting. Knowledge of a public player identifier alone may not establish ownership; we may request additional non-sensitive information to verify that you are authorized.

We will respond without undue delay in accordance with applicable law, explain any further information needed, arrange applicable provider deletion requests, and confirm the outcome once complete.

⚠️

Irreversible Action: Deleted game progress may be unrecoverable. Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile. Manage those accounts directly through Google.

3. Storage & Separation

Local device saves and cloud backups operate independently:

On Device

Local Save Progress

Uninstalling the Game, clearing Android app storage, or signing out does not by itself delete cloud records. Deleting cloud records does not remotely clear your device.

Clear locally via: Android Settings → Apps → Dessert Merge Cafe → Storage → Clear storage. This removes local progress and privacy choices.

Cloud Storage

Cloud Records & Account

Unity Authentication, Cloud Save backups, and leaderboard entries reside in cloud infrastructure. Requesting account deletion initiates permanent backend erasure.

After requesting account deletion, avoid continuing to play or signing in again until support confirms the outcome; using the Game again can generate new data.

4. Retention Exceptions

We may retain limited information when necessary for legal obligations, security/fraud prevention, or dispute handling, including an appropriate record that a request was fulfilled. We will explain relevant exceptions in our response.

Provider systems and backups can require additional processing time to complete full erasure.

Withdrawing optional analytics consent in the Game's Settings stops future collection, but does not itself erase previously collected historical data; historical analytics records are deleted through this deletion request process.
